## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a forward-looking, citizen-first civic ethos. Built for modern public infrastructure, the visual tone blends ecological stewardship with national pride, technological clarity, and administrative transparency. The emotional signature is optimistic, pristine, and authoritative without feeling bureaucratic. 

The aesthetic style merges **Modern Glassmorphism** with **Clean Minimalism**:
- Translucent surface panels that echo pure water and unpolluted air.
- Rich, luminous ambient backdrops where deep blues, pure teals, solar ambers, and lucid cyans blend subtly.
- Uncluttered screen composition allowing quick citizen onboarding, grievance logging, and field verification.
- Crisp, high-contrast actions to ensure absolute usability under harsh outdoor sunlight on handheld mobile devices.

## Colors

The palette draws from environmental vitality and civic dignity:
- **Primary (`#002E87`)**: Deep Civic Blue representing institutional trust, authority, and administrative reliability. Used for primary CTAs, active indicators, and high-priority civic confirmation states.
- **Secondary (`#83dbe4`)**: Fresh Aqua Cyan, anchoring the portal with institutional clarity, water sanitation, and technological precision.
- **Tertiary (`#D97706`)**: Solar Amber/Gold, echoing radiant energy, civic recognition badges, alerts, and verification highlights.
- **Cyan Accent (`#06B6D4`)**: Reserved for atmospheric background gradients, dynamic focus rings, and high-visibility status pings.
- **Neutral (`#0F172A`)**: Slate 900 for high-contrast, crystal-clear typographic rendering over bright or translucent backings.
- **Canvas (`#F8FAFC`)**: A pristine, cool-tinted off-white foundation that guarantees clean readability and eliminates glare.

### Atmospheric Gradients
- **Civic Aurora (Auth & Hero)**: Linear 135deg gradient from `#002E87` through `#83dbe4` into hints of `#06B6D4` with ambient accents of `#F59E0B` (opacity 12% to 24%).
- **Glass Tint**: 60%–80% opacity white with subtle teal/cyan border refractions (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` over `rgba(131, 219, 228, 0.12)`).

## Typography

Typography relies on **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all levels. Its balanced geometry, open counters, and humanistic touch offer extreme legibility on low-resolution screens while communicating contemporary sophistication.

- **Scale & Contrast**: Headlines leverage weights `700` and `800` with subtle negative tracking for punchy, decisive messaging. Body copy remains at weight `400` with generous line-heights to guarantee ease of reading for citizens of all age groups.
- **Numbers & Multi-language Support**: Digits are crisp and uniform for mobile OTP inputs, ward numbers, and civic metric counters.
- **Labels & Forms**: Form field descriptors and verification badges employ weight `600` with controlled letter-spacing to prevent scanning fatigue during multi-step logins and registrations.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is governed by an 8pt base grid system designed for accessibility and density modulation:
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: Fluid single-column view anchored within a 390px–430px optimal viewport container. Centered card layouts with 20px horizontal page margins and 16px component gutters ensure one-handed thumb reach for authentication fields and social/citizen SSO buttons.
- **Tablet & Desktop (≥ 768px)**: Split-screen composition. The primary side (55% width) hosts an expressive gradient canvas showcasing civic sanitation impact, animated community metrics, and official seal badges. The secondary side (45% width, max 480px content box) holds the glassmorphic authentication shell.
- **Rhythm**: Component interiors enforce strict inner paddings (`space-md` for inputs, `space-xl` for modal card wrappers) to prevent clutter and maintain visual breathing room.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses layered glassmorphism combined with solar-blue tinted ambient drop shadows:

- **Level 0 (Foundation)**: Pristine background canvas (`#F8FAFC`) textured with subtle blurred organic shapes of blue and cyan.
- **Level 1 (Glass Surface)**: Translucent login card containers with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, a background fill of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.82)`, and a fine 1px border colored `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.60)` on the top/left edges and `rgba(131, 219, 228, 0.15)` on the bottom/right edges.
- **Level 2 (Active Elements & Modals)**: Elevated inputs and popovers using an ambient shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 46, 135, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(131, 219, 228, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Action States & Hover)**: Interactive primary buttons and floating language selectors:
  `box-shadow: 0 16px 28px -6px rgba(0, 46, 135, 0.35), 0 6px 10px -2px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.20)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Rounded (Level 2)** shape identity to communicate warmth, modern civic hospitality, and safety:
- **Default Elements (`0.5rem / 8px`)**: Checkboxes, OTP cell fields, inline tags, and mini chips.
- **Cards & Primary Modules (`1rem / 16px`)**: Authentication cards, modal panels, and alert banners.
- **Interactive Buttons & Badges (`1.5rem / 24px` or Full Pill)**: Primary CTA buttons, verification chips, and language toggles utilize full pill structures to maximize touch affordance and eliminate harsh mechanical corners.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: Deep blue-to-aqua gradient background (`linear-gradient(135deg, #002E87 0%, #83dbe4 100%)`), text color `#FFFFFF`, bold weight (`700`), full pill radius. On hover, deepens with a solar amber rim glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 2px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.6)`). Minimum touch target: 48px height.
- **Secondary Button**: Crisp white glass background (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`), 1.5px solid border in `#83dbe4`, label color `#002E87`.
- **Citizen Service Button (Aadhaar / MeriPehchan SSO)**: Clean white surface with subtle neutral border (`#CBD5E1`), displaying authoritative branding icons with high contrast labels.

### Input Fields & OTP Cells
- **Text Inputs**: 48px height, rounded-lg (`12px`), background `#FFFFFF` with 1px border (`#E2E8F0`). Label sits outside with `label-md` styling in `#334155`.
- **Focus State**: 2px border in `#002E87` accompanied by a soft halo: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 4px rgba(131, 219, 228, 0.2)`.
- **OTP Segmented Cells**: 56px height × 48px width individual boxes, center-aligned `headline-md` type, automatic active transitions with solar amber cursor indicators.

### Cards
- **Auth Glass Container**: Encased with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`, `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` fill, and dual directional highlight borders. Generous internal padding (32px mobile, 40px desktop).
- **Metric Badges**: Mini cards featuring solar amber icons, bold counting labels, and micro captions celebrating local cleanliness achievements.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Indicator**: Pill shaped, semi-translucent primary blue fill (`rgba(0, 46, 135, 0.1)`) with `#002E87` text and a pulsing 6px dot.
- **Role Selector Chips (Citizen, Sanitation Worker, Admin)**: Toggleable chips with subtle borders that transition to solid `#002E87` with white text on selection.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Civic Consent Checkboxes**: 20px × 20px rounded-sm (`4px`) frame. Unchecked: `#CBD5E1` border. Checked: `#002E87` solid fill with white checkmark icon.
